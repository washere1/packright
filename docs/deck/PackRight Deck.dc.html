01 — The problem

The Overweight Bag Problem

Packing is guesswork. You learn the real weight at the check-in scale, and whether it fits only when the zipper won't close.

01 — The problem

What Overpacking Costs

Money
Overweight fees Charged per bag, per leg, at the counter with no way to undo.
Waste
Binned at the gate Toiletries, clothes and gifts dumped to make the limit end up as airport landfill.
Time
Slower queues Every repack at the counter holds up the whole line and the airline's staff.

03 — The engine

The Packing Engine

1 · Gemini understands Reads the scan, suggests handling metadata Fragility · stacking · access · liquid risk · orientations. Advisory only — you confirm.
→
2 · Solver packs Deterministic selection + 3D placement Highest-priority feasible subset under the allowance · collision-free, supported placements · heavy toward wheels, quick-access near the lid
→
3 · Validator checks Independently rebuilds and verifies the plan Bounds, collisions, support, weight, sequence. Only then is it marked feasible — with numbered instructions and a 3D viewer.
