# Two-minute judge demo

1. Open the local app and select the curated demo items.
2. Show the selection tray, suitcase constraints, and advisory preflight.
3. Create a plan and wait for the persisted job to complete.
4. Use the 3D viewer stepper to inspect the first three placements, orientation, depth, and approximate center of mass.
5. Replan with a narrower suitcase; keep the baseline visible while the new job runs.
6. Show the failed/search-exhausted evidence if the narrower constraint cannot fit the mandatory item.
7. Reload the plan URL and open Engineering details to show the snapshot and solver seed.

The viewer is a box-based planning aid. It does not certify physical fit or insertion paths.
